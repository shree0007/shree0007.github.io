About Me

Hello Everyone 👋 I'm Shree, an Environmental Engineering graduate with a Master’s in Circular Economy, currently working as an EHS Officer on Microsoft data centre construction projects. My interests include EHS management, Life Cycle Assessment (LCA), carbon footprint analysis, and sustainability. Throughout my academic and professional journey, I’ve been passionate about EHS, sustainability, and developing practical solutions for a safer and more sustainable future.

My Bachelor's degree in Environmental Engineering, specializing in water and waste management from Metropolia University of Applied Sciences, provided me with a solid foundation in environmental sciences. My thesis, titled 'Study of Water Quality in Helsinki’s Urban Streams: Mätäjoki and Haaganpuro,' strengthened my skills in water quality monitoring. During my internship at Ledika Oy, I researched algae growth in urine-based media for sustainable biomass production, conducting literature reviews and lab experiments to explore circular nutrient cycles and renewable energy. My Master's degree in Environmental Technology with a specialization in Circular Economy from LUT University deepened my knowledge in Circular Economy, Sustainability, Climate Change, Carbon Footprint, and Life Cycle Assessment. My thesis on the carbon footprint of polyethylene produced from CO₂ and renewable H₂ strengthened my skills in LCA methodology, ISO standards, and environmental impact assessment.

Currently, I work as an EHS Officer at Direct Strike International on multiple Microsoft data centre construction projects, supporting EHS compliance, inspections, audits, risk assessments, and documentation. I use digital platforms such as Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC), Raken, and Site Command for EHS reporting and management. Previously, I worked as a Senior Production and Quality Control Specialist at Swappie Oy, contributing to circular economy goals by extending product lifespans and reducing electronic waste. I also hold a Vocational Qualification in Information and Communications Technology, specializing in Full Stack Development. During my internship at Druid Oy, I gained experience in software development and maintenance. Additionally, I have developed data analysis skills using SQL, Excel, Tableau, and Power BI.

I am motivated to apply my background in environmental engineering, circular economy, and EHS management to support safer and more sustainable practices. My academic and professional experience is relevant to EHS and sustainability-related projects. In particular, I am interested in EHS management, sustainability, life cycle assessment, environmental impact assessment, and carbon footprint analysis, while remaining open to opportunities across the broader fields of EHS, sustainability, and circular economy. I am committed to continuous learning and professional development and welcome opportunities for collaboration and knowledge sharing.

Beyond work, I enjoy immersing myself in nature, sauna sessions, and playing football, billiards, and ping pong. Let’s connect 🤝 and explore the intersection of EHS, sustainability, technology, and data-driven solutions!
