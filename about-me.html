About Me

Hello Everyone 👋 I'm Shree, an Environmental Engineering graduate with a Master’s in Circular Economy. My expertise lies in sustainability, with specialized skills in Life Cycle Assessment (LCA), carbon footprint analysis, and environmental impact assessment. Throughout my academic and professional journey, I’ve been deeply interested in analyzing environmental impacts and developing sustainable solutions.

My Bachelor's degree in Environmental Engineering, specializing in water and waste management from Metropolia University of Applied Sciences, provided me with a solid foundation in environmental sciences. My thesis, titled 'Study of Water Quality in Helsinki’s Urban Streams: Mätäjoki and Haaganpuro,' strengthened my skills in water quality monitoring. During my internship at Ledika Oy, I researched algae growth in urine-based media for sustainable biomass production, conducting literature reviews and lab experiments to explore circular nutrient cycles and renewable energy. My Master's degree in Environmental Technology with a specialization in Circular Economy from LUT University deepened my knowledge in key areas like Circular Economy, Sustainability, Climate Change, Carbon Footprint, and Life Cycle Assessment. My thesis on the carbon footprint of polyethylene produced from CO₂ and renewable H₂ equipped me with advanced skills in LCA methodology, ISO standards, and environmental impact assessment.

In my recent role as Senior Production Specialist at Swappie Oy, I contributed to achieving circular economy goals within the tech sector. This role enhanced my understanding of sustainability practices and allowed me to apply my knowledge in a business setting. I also hold a Vocational Qualification in Information and Communications Technology, specializing in Full Stack Development, which has broadened my technical expertise. As an intern at Druid Oy, I contributed to software maintenance and optimization efforts, focusing on enhancing efficiency and supporting digital sustainability. Furthermore, I am actively developing my data analysis skills with tools such as SQL, Excel, Tableau, and Power BI, and I am pursuing certifications in sustainability and ESG to stay aligned with industry best practices.

I am motivated to apply my background in environmental engineering and circular economy to support sustainability and circularity efforts. My academic and technical experience is relevant to a range of environmental sustainability-related projects. In particular, I am interested in work related to life cycle assessment, environmental impact assessment, and GHG and carbon footprint analysis, while also being open to contributing to other projects across the broader areas of environment, sustainability, and circular economy. I am eager to continue developing my skills and would welcome the opportunity to learn through practical collaboration.

Beyond work, I enjoy immersing myself in nature, sauna sessions, and playing football, billiards, and ping pong. Let’s connect 🤝 and explore the intersection of sustainability, technology, and data-driven solutions!
